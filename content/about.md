Hi, I'm **Maciej Barelkowski**. I work at [Camunda](https://camunda.com) on [bpmn.io](https://bpmn.io), the open-source toolkits for viewing and editing BPMN, DMN and forms in the browser, and on the [Camunda Modeler](https://github.com/camunda/camunda-modeler) desktop app built on them. I live in Poznań, Poland.

## Side projects

- [asdf-camunda-modeler](https://github.com/barmac/asdf-camunda-modeler): install and switch Camunda Modeler versions with the asdf version manager.
- [discourse-bpmn-io](https://github.com/barmac/discourse-bpmn-io): render BPMN and DMN diagrams attached to Discourse posts.
- [jsdmn](https://github.com/barmac/jsdmn): a DMN 1.3 interpreter for JavaScript.
- [bpmn-js-lasso-group](https://github.com/barmac/bpmn-js-lasso-group): draw a BPMN group with a lasso-like tool.
- Camunda Modeler plugins: [alternative icons](https://github.com/barmac/camunda-modeler-alternative-icons-plugin) for templated elements, [debounce settings](https://github.com/barmac/camunda-modeler-debounce-plugin) and [one-click restart](https://github.com/barmac/camunda-modeler-restart-plugin).
- [oh-my-npm-run](https://github.com/barmac/oh-my-npm-run) and [oh-my-npm-link](https://github.com/barmac/oh-my-npm-link): list `npm run` scripts and autocomplete `npm link` targets in your shell.
- [zoom-now](https://github.com/barmac/zoom-now) and [film-ratings](https://github.com/barmac/film-ratings): small browser extensions, one to open Zoom links straight in the desktop app and one to show FilmWeb ratings on Netflix and HBO Go.
- [msfroggenerator2](https://github.com/barmac/msfroggenerator2): my write-up of a PicoCTF 2023 web challenge, solved with team Cwiercglowki.
